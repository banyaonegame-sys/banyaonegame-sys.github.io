Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

한국어 보기

banyaonegame ("we", "the developer") operates the mobile app "Learn Korean with Natives" (the "App"). This policy explains what information the App handles and why.

1. Information we collect

  • No account required: the App has no sign-up or login. We do not collect your name, email address, or contacts.
  • Learning progress and settings (stages cleared, selected native language, level) are stored only on your device, in encrypted form. They are not sent to our servers.
  • Purchase information: when you buy the Lifetime Premium item, we store the order ID, product ID, purchase state, purchase time, and a one-way hash of the purchase token. Payment is handled by Google Play; we never receive your card details.
  • Advertising ID (Android advertising ID) and device information, collected by Google AdMob to show ads to free users.
  • Network and device information (IP address, device model, OS version), processed by Google services when the App connects to them.
  • App integrity information: Firebase App Check uses Google Play Integrity to confirm requests come from the genuine App.

2. How we use it

  • To verify purchases with Google Play and prevent fraudulent purchases.
  • To show ads (interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads) to users without Premium. Premium users do not see ads.
  • To protect our server from abuse (App Check).
  • To answer your inquiries.

3. Third-party services

We do not sell your information. The App uses the following services provided by Google LLC:

  • Google Play Billing – payments
  • Firebase (Cloud Functions, Cloud Firestore, App Check) – purchase verification and server protection
  • Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform – ads and consent management

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Your choices

  • Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and can change their choice in the App.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads (menu names may vary by device).
  • Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes all learning progress stored on your device.

5. Retention and deletion

Learning data exists only on your device. Purchase records are kept for 5 years as required by the Korean Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, then destroyed. To request deletion of your purchase record, email us with your Google Play order ID (GPA.xxxx).

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when the App or the law changes. Changes are posted on this page.
