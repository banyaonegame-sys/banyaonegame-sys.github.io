Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

한국어 보기

banyaonegame ("we", "the developer") operates the mobile app "HanQuest: Learn Korean" (the "App", formerly "Learn Korean with Natives"). This policy explains what information the App handles and why.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: you can start as a guest or sign in with Google or with an email address and password. We use Firebase Authentication for this.
    • Guest: an anonymous account ID is created. No name or email is collected.
    • Google or email sign-in: your account ID and email address. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store them.
  • Learning progress and settings (stages cleared, selected native language, level, text size) are stored only on your device, in encrypted form. They are not sent to our servers.
  • Usage statistics: once per app launch, the App sends your account ID together with the sign-in type (guest, Google, email), device type (Android, iPhone), the country/region setting of your phone, and your phone's language. We use this only to count daily and monthly users. It does not include your email address or name.
  • Sign-up survey: on first launch we may ask "How did you find HanQuest?". Your answer (for example "YouTube" or "Skip") is stored with your account ID.
  • Lifetime-free membership: if the developer grants your account free access, your account ID, email address, and a short note are stored.
  • Purchase information: when you buy the Lifetime Pass, we store the order ID, product ID, purchase state, purchase time, the price and currency shown in the App, and a one-way hash of the purchase token. Payment is handled by Google Play (or the App Store); we never receive your card details.
  • Advertising ID (Android advertising ID) and device information, collected by Google AdMob to show ads to free users.
  • Network and device information (IP address, device model, OS version), processed by Google services when the App connects to them.
  • App integrity information: Firebase App Check uses Google Play Integrity to confirm requests come from the genuine App.

2. How we use it

  • To let you sign in and to manage your account (including account deletion).
  • To verify purchases with Google Play and prevent fraudulent purchases.
  • To give lifetime-free access to accounts chosen by the developer.
  • To understand how many people use the App and how they found it (statistics).
  • To show ads (interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads) to users without Premium. Premium users do not see ads.
  • To protect our server from abuse (App Check).
  • To answer your inquiries.

3. Third-party services

We do not sell your information. The App uses the following services provided by Google LLC:

  • Google Play Billing – payments
  • Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Functions, Cloud Firestore, App Check) – sign-in, purchase verification, statistics, and server protection
  • Google Sign-In (Credential Manager) – signing in with your Google account
  • Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform – ads and consent management

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Your choices

  • You can use the App as a guest without giving an email address.
  • You can skip the sign-up survey.
  • Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and can change their choice in the App (Account → Privacy settings).
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads (menu names may vary by device).

5. Account deletion

  • In the App: tap Account at the bottom of the stage screen → Delete account → Delete. Your account is deleted immediately.
  • On the web: https://banyaonegame-sys.github.io/hanquest-delete-account.html, or email us. We delete the account within 7 days after confirming it is yours.
  • What is deleted: your account (account ID and email address), lifetime-free membership, sign-up survey answer, and visit records linked to your account ID. Learning progress on the phone is reset.

6. Retention

  • Account information is kept until you delete your account.
  • Learning data exists only on your device and is removed when you delete your account in the App or uninstall the App.
  • Purchase records (which do not include your account ID or email) are kept for 5 years as required by the Korean Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, then destroyed.
  • Statistics totals (numbers only, no personal information) may be kept.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the App or the law changes. Changes are posted on this page.

  • November 23, 2026: app renamed to HanQuest; added sign-in (guest, Google, email), usage statistics, sign-up survey, lifetime-free membership, and account deletion
  • November 11, 2026: first version
