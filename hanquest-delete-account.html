HanQuest: Learn Korean — Delete your account

Developer: banyaonegame (반야원) · App: HanQuest: Learn Korean (com.banyaonegame.koreanrpg)

Option 1. Delete in the app

  1. Open HanQuest.
  2. On the stage screen, tap Account at the bottom center.
  3. Tap Delete account, read the notice, and tap Delete.
  4. Your account is deleted right away and the app closes.

Option 2. Request by email

If you removed the app or cannot sign in, send an email with the details below.

  • To: banyaonegame@gmail.com
  • Subject: [HanQuest] Account deletion request
  • Content: the email address you signed in with (Google or email account)

We delete the account within 7 days after confirming it is yours, and reply with the result.

Data that is deleted

  • Account: sign-in account ID and email address
  • Data linked to the account on our server: lifetime-free member status, sign-up survey answer, visit records
  • Learning progress is stored only on your phone and is reset when you delete the account in the app (or when you uninstall the app).

Data that is kept

  • Purchase verification records (no account ID or email) are kept for 5 years for refunds and accounting, as required by law, and then destroyed.
  • Statistics counts (numbers only, no personal information).
  • Payment details are managed by Google Play; the developer does not store them. A lifetime pass bought on Google Play can be restored with the same Google account.
